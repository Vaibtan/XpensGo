What Xpensego does

Capture without discipline

Paste one bank SMS or fifty, drop a CSV statement, or just type "chai 30, auto 80" for cash. You never owe Xpensego a habit — feed it whatever you already have, whenever you remember.

Categorization that's actually right

It knows a Blinkit grocery run from a Zomato dinner. It asks once what a UPI transfer to a person was for — and never asks about that payee again. Say "no, that's groceries" and the correction sticks forever.

Answers and warnings, not dashboards

Ask anything about your money and get the number first, in one message. Type "food budget 5000" and it's set — Xpensego messages you at 80% before the budget blows. There is no app to open.
